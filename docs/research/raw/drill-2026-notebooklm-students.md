Upload your sources

Upload all of your notes, lecture recordings, or any other class materials to Gemini Notebook.

![Image 2: Upload your sources](https://notebook.google/_/static/v4/sources.png)

Answers with citations

Ask questions about your sources and get detailed responses with citations. Summarize lengthy, complex information.

![Image 3: Ask questions](https://notebook.google/_/static/v4/ask-questions.png)

Create study materials

Understand any topic with Audio Overviews, Mind Maps, or Slide Decks generated from your sources.

![Image 4: Create study materials](https://notebook.google/_/static/v4/audio-overview-2x.png)

Review and retain

Review your materials with custom flashcards and personalized quizzes to help you understand every concept.

![Image 5: Review and retain](https://notebook.google/_/static/v4/review-retain-module.png)

Flashcards

Generate flashcards from your documents, practice active learning, and ask Notebook for detailed explanations to master key concepts.

Quizzes

Create personalized quizzes from your sources, with detailed explanations for incorrect answers.

Slide Decks

Transform your study materials into organized, visually appealing slide decks to review main points and structure your learning.

Audio Overviews

Create a podcast-like audio summary where two AI hosts discuss, debate, and summarize your study materials.

Video Overviews

Instantly transform your documents into narrated videos to visually grasp key ideas.

Mind Maps

Create an auto-generated mind map detailing all the connections across your sources.

AI-suggested Reports

Generate various high-quality documents like blog posts or critiques, with dynamic suggestions and custom formats based on the sources.

Q&A

Start a Q&A in Chat with citations against your sources.

We value your privacy and never use your organization's data to train Gemini Notebook

As an organization or school, your data will stay private to you. When you use Gemini Notebook as an individual, your data is not used for training unless you share feedback, [see more details here](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16179536?sjid=6202054122524519860-NC).
